About the project

Science behind BioPredict

An end-to-end cheminformatics pipeline — from curated ChEMBL bioassays to an explainable, deployable web service.

Project Overview & Biological Rationale

Targeting Cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2) for Next-Generation Anti-Inflammatory Leads

Cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2, gene PTGS2) is an inducible enzyme that converts arachidonic acid into pro-inflammatory prostaglandins. Classical non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs) non-selectively inhibit both COX-1 and COX-2. Because constitutive COX-1 maintains gastric mucosal protection and platelet homeostasis, non-selective inhibition frequently triggers severe gastrointestinal ulceration and bleeding.

BioPredict was developed to accelerate the computational discovery of selective COX-2 inhibitors. By exploiting the distinct secondary hydrophilic binding pocket of COX-2 (formed by Val523 replacing Ile523 in COX-1), our predictive models prioritize compounds with high selectivity anchors, reducing bench attrition and animal testing.

COX-2 & ChEMBL Dataset

6,240 bioactivity records curated from ChEMBL (CHEMBL230), filtered for Homo sapiens assays, deduplicated, and binarized using a strict pharmacological cutoff (IC50 ≤ 1,000 nM = Active).

Morgan Fingerprints

Molecules are translated into 2048-bit circular Extended Connectivity Fingerprints (ECFP4 equivalent, radius=2) with RDKit, capturing intricate atom neighborhood environments.

XGBoost Classifier

Extreme Gradient Boosting trees selected after empirical benchmarking across 6 ML architectures, yielding 82.21% cross-validation accuracy and superior generalization on holdout tests.

Explainable AI (XAI)

Gini split importance scores are matched back to bit-origin atom environments, highlighting key pharmacophores (such as sulfonamide and CF3 groups) directly in interactive 3D.
